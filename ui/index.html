Getting the token
Xiaomi heaters only accept local commands signed with a per-device token, which is stored in your Xiaomi account.
  1. Download token_extractor.exe from github.com/PiotrMachowski/Xiaomi-cloud-tokens-extractor (Releases).
  2. Run it and log in with your Xiaomi Home account (same region as in the app).
  3. Find your heater in the list (model xiaomi.heater.ma8) and copy its IP and TOKEN here.
